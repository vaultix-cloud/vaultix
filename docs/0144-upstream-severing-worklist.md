Produced by a read-only trace of the tree at `main` 9116b650: 7 independent lenses, then 2 skeptics per finding (one on reachability, one on
egress or residue), then a completeness critic. 157 agents, no edits. Line numbers and counts come from agents reading the files; they are
accurate to the commit above and approximate where marked. Nothing here has been run against a live image yet: "default on" is a reading
of the code and its defaults, and step 0 and step 1 below exist to turn it into a measurement.

Why it matters: Vaultix must keep every capability the base application has (docs/0026), while the Infisical connection and licence are cut
(docs/0015, docs/0127). Cutting without a measured before-state would be guessing.

Classification rule applied to the input: CONFIRMED means both skeptic verdicts (reach and egress) are true. DISPUTED means the verdicts split. REFUTED means both are false. "Default on?" is as the skeptics resolved it.

Only three outbound paths made by the product itself are default-on: the browser HIBP call, the server update check (tagged builds only) and the CLI update notice (tagged builds only). The critic found no new default-on connection in kernel/, panel/, platform/shippin or the Go sidecar. The rest is opt-in egress, dead code, build/deploy-time fetches, or upstream residue.
